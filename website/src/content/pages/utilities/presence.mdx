## Web Components

`@ark-ui/web` has no standalone presence element. Presence-aware parts, such as dialog and popover content, keep their
content mounted for exit animations. Put content in a `<template>` inside the part to mount it lazily, and add
`unmount-on-exit` to the root to remove it after it exits. See the
[Animation guide](/docs/guides/animation#web-components).

</FrameworkOnly>
